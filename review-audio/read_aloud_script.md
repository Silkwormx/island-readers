# Read-aloud script

Wording review before any voice is built. This is what the app speaks today. It does not change the app.

Frill is the voice chosen for the later build. These lines are the script to correct first. Isolated phonemes stay out of that voice build.

Rows: 1044. Lines flagged `uses letter name` where a sound was intended: 14.

Notes:

- `uses letter name` means the spoken text is a letter name, or starts with a single letter, where the child should hear a sound.
- `letter name, intended` is a name tap, or the silent-e rule, and should stay a name.
- `isolated phoneme` is a sound by itself. Device speech cannot say these reliably. They need a human recording, not Frill.
- `device TTS often says the letter name` is a short-vowel spelling such as ah, eh, ih, or uh. The letters on the page are a sound attempt, and the device voice usually says the name.

Order is the path a child takes: Home, Foundations, Units, game directions, stories, then sight words.

Counts by area: Home 2, Foundations 265, Units 330, Games 72, Stories 79, Sight words 296.
