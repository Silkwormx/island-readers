# Foundations lesson read-aloud script

Wording review before any voice is recorded. These are the narration lines in the student lessons. The app still speaks them with the device voice already in the app. No new audio file is added here.

Frill is the v4 B nudibranch (`img/frill.webp`). She says every instruction. Zip is the green gecko (`img/zipA.webp`). He says the praise line. Each line shows that character's picture beside the words.

Sound buttons and word taps are not character lines. They keep the existing device speech: a pure sound such as ah, mmm, or sss, or the word itself. They are not listed below and they are not a new recording.
